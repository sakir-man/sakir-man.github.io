Research specialization: **Islamic Astronomy · Crescent Visibility · Twilight (*Syafaq*) · Hisab–Rukyat · Hijri Calendar · Islamic Legal Studies**

## Research interests

- Crescent visibility and twilight phenomena
- Observational astronomy and lunar photometry
- Hisab–rukyat and Hijri calendar systems
- Qibla direction and prayer-time studies
- Integration of Islamic law and astronomical science
- Islamic legal methodology and *maqāṣid al-sharī‘ah*
- Contemporary Islamic family law

## Academic and editorial activities

- Lecturer in Islamic law and Islamic astronomy

- Reviewer for academic journals in Islamic law and Islamic astronomy
- Researcher and trainer in crescent observation, qibla, prayer times, and Hijri calendar studies
