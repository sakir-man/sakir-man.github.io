I am a lecturer at the Faculty of Sharia and the Graduate Program of **UIN Jurai Siwo Lampung, Indonesia**. My research lies at the intersection of **Islamic law and Islamic astronomy (Ilmu Falak)**, with particular emphasis on crescent visibility, twilight (*syafaq*), hisab–rukyat, and the development of Hijri calendar systems.

My current research investigates how **twilight sky brightness, atmospheric conditions, lunar geometry, and digital imaging** affect the visibility of the young crescent Moon. By combining observational astronomy, photometric analysis, numerical modeling, and Islamic legal reasoning, I aim to develop empirically grounded approaches to crescent visibility and calendar determination, particularly for tropical and low-latitude environments.

Alongside this main research program, I work on **Islamic legal studies**, including contemporary Islamic family law, *maqāṣid al-sharī‘ah*, and the integration of scientific evidence into Islamic legal reasoning. I am also actively involved in teaching, academic publishing, journal editing, and community-based education in Islamic astronomy and Sharia studies.
