My teaching spans Islamic law, philosophy of science, Islamic family law, Sharia administration, and Islamic astronomy at undergraduate and postgraduate levels.

## Philosophy of Science and Integration of Knowledge

Graduate Program, Islamic Family Law.  
The course examines philosophy of science, paradigms of knowledge, and contemporary Muslim approaches to integration and Islamization of knowledge. It encourages students to connect revelation, reason, empirical knowledge, and social reality in the study of Islamic law.

## Philosophy of Islamic Family Law

Graduate Program, Islamic Family Law.  
This course explores the philosophical foundations, values, purposes, and contemporary development of Islamic family law, including marriage, divorce, lineage, inheritance, justice, and *maqāṣid al-sharī‘ah*.

## Sharia Administration Practicum

Faculty of Sharia, Islamic Family Law.  
Students study the institutional practice of the Office of Religious Affairs (KUA) and Religious Courts, including marriage administration, family-law services, case administration, and practical legal procedures.

## Advanced Islamic Astronomy

Faculty of Sharia, Islamic Family Law.  
The course covers astronomical foundations and their application to Hijri calendar calculation, crescent visibility, rukyat, qibla direction, prayer times, and the use of contemporary observational instruments and software.
