Teaching

Courses connecting philosophy of science, Islamic family law, institutional practice, and advanced Islamic astronomy.

Selected courses

Critical, integrative, and practice-oriented learning.

Graduate · Islamic Family Law

Filsafat Ilmu dan Integrasi Keilmuan

Philosophy of science, scientific paradigms, and integration of knowledge in relation to religion, social reality, and Islamic family law.

Graduate · Islamic Family Law

Filsafat Hukum Keluarga Islam

Philosophical foundations, values, and rationality behind marriage, divorce, lineage, inheritance, and contemporary family-law issues.

Faculty of Sharia · Undergraduate

Teaching approach

From concepts to evidence and practice.

01

Conceptual foundations

Students first master the philosophical, legal, or astronomical concepts that structure the field.

02

Case & evidence analysis

Learning then moves to documents, observations, data, and real institutional cases.

03

Scholarly communication

Assignments emphasize argumentation, academic writing, presentation, and research-oriented discussion.
