Dr. Sakirman

I am a lecturer at the Faculty of Sharia and the Graduate Program of UIN Jurai Siwo Lampung. My academic work connects Islamic law with Islamic astronomy, particularly lunar observation, crescent visibility, twilight phenomena, and Islamic calendar systems.

My research examines how empirical astronomical evidence can contribute to contemporary Islamic legal reasoning. I am especially interested in the relationship between hisab and rukyat, the visibility of the young crescent under tropical twilight conditions, and the development of scientifically robust yet jurisprudentially legitimate criteria for the Hijri calendar.

Alongside research, I teach undergraduate and postgraduate courses in Islamic law, Islamic family law, philosophy of science and knowledge integration, and advanced Islamic astronomy. I also contribute to academic publishing and scholarly service, including editorial work in Islamic law.
