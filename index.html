About

Bridging normative reasoning and empirical observation.

Dr. Sakirman is a lecturer at the Faculty of Sharia and the Graduate Program of UIN Jurai Siwo Lampung. His academic work connects Islamic law with Islamic astronomy, particularly lunar observation, crescent visibility, twilight phenomena, and Islamic calendar systems.

He completed undergraduate studies in Islamic Law at UIN Sunan Kalijaga Yogyakarta, a Master’s degree in Islamic Studies at IAIN Walisongo Semarang, and a Ph.D. in Islamic Studies at UIN Walisongo Semarang through the Ministry of Religious Affairs’ 5000 Doctoral Scholarship Program.
