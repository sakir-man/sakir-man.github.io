## Key questions

- How does twilight sky brightness (*syafaq*) affect the minimum detectable contrast between the young crescent and the background sky?
- How do atmospheric conditions and lunar geometry interact in determining crescent visibility in tropical environments?
- How can digital imaging and photometric analysis improve the reliability of hilal detection?
- How should empirical visibility evidence be incorporated into contemporary hisab–rukyat criteria and Hijri calendar systems?
- How can Islamic legal reasoning respond to probabilistic, data-driven, and technology-assisted crescent observations?

My research connects **observational astronomy and Islamic legal reasoning**. The central objective is to understand the physical conditions under which the young crescent becomes detectable and to examine how this evidence can contribute to contemporary discussions of rukyat, hisab, and Hijri calendar determination.

I use field observations, digital imaging, aperture photometry, sky-brightness measurements, and numerical modeling to investigate the relationship between the crescent and the twilight background. Particular attention is given to tropical and low-latitude environments, where atmospheric conditions can differ from those represented in many existing visibility models.

# Current research themes

## Twilight (*Syafaq*) and crescent visibility
This research examines twilight brightness as a key background condition for crescent detection. It focuses on contrast, atmospheric extinction, sky brightness, and the transition from twilight to darker sky conditions.

## Observational astronomy and photometry
I use observational data and digital image analysis to quantify crescent intensity, background sky intensity, and visibility contrast. This line of work seeks reproducible empirical indicators that complement visual reports.

## Hisab–rukyat and Hijri calendar criteria
This theme investigates how astronomical parameters and observational evidence can be integrated into contemporary criteria for the beginning of Hijri months, including questions of uncertainty, regional applicability, and calendar unification.

## Islamic law and astronomical evidence
I examine the jurisprudential implications of scientific evidence in rukyat and calendar determination: the authority of observation, the role of calculation, the evaluation of conflicting reports, and the legal status of technology-assisted detection.

## Technology and future observation systems
My future-oriented work considers digital imaging, standardized observation protocols, citizen science, and AI-assisted detection as tools for improving transparency, reproducibility, and public trust in crescent observation.
